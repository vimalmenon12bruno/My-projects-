# Format Templates + Swipe Files

Fill-in-the-blank templates for every Bright Side format, plus a hook bank and a title
bank so you can spin up new videos fast. Brackets `[ ]` are the parts you swap.

---

## Template A — Listicle / Countdown

> Best for: body facts, life hacks, science facts, "things you didn't know."

```
[COLD OPEN — 0:00]
VO: Right now, [most surprising single claim stated as fact]. And that's not even
    the strangest one on this list.

[PROMISE — 0:15]
VO: Here are [N] [topic] facts that sound fake — but are 100% real. Stick around,
    because number one is the one nobody believes. Let's start with number [N].

[ITEM LOOP — repeat for each item, ~25–40s each]
TEXT: #[n] · [3-word caption]
VO (mini-hook):  Ever wondered [curiosity-gap question about the item]?
VO (reveal):     [The fact / payload.]
VO (amplifier):  [A comparison, stat, or twist that makes it feel huge.]
VO (bridge):     But that's nothing compared to number [n-1]…

[MID RE-HOOK — ~halfway]
VO: We're halfway there, and the craziest one is still coming. But first…

[PAYOFF — #1]
VO: And here it is. The one we had to fact-check three times. [Biggest fact.]

[OUTRO]
VO: Which one surprised you most? Tell us in the comments. Give this a like, share it
    with a friend, and click one of the videos on your screen. Stay on the Bright
    Side of life!
```

## Template B — Riddle / Test

> Best for: "Only X% can pass," illusions, brain teasers. Highest shareability.

```
[COLD OPEN]
VO: Studies say only [X]% of people can solve all of these. Do you think you're one
    of them? Let's find out.

[PROMISE]
VO: I'll show you [N] riddles. Each one gets harder. Pause if you need to — and keep
    track of your score. Ready? Here's number one.

[RIDDLE LOOP — repeat]
TEXT: RIDDLE #[n]
VO (setup):     [Read the riddle clearly, once.]
VO (prompt):    Take a second and think about it.
[COUNTDOWN VISUAL: 3… 2… 1… — hold a real 3–5s pause with ticking SFX]
VO (answer):    The answer is… [answer]. Did you get it?
VO (bridge):    Give yourself a point if you did. Now it gets trickier…

[SCORE-OUT]
VO: So — how many did you get? 0 to 3, you're a dreamer. 4 to 7, sharp mind.
    8 or more? You're a genuine genius. Tell us your score in the comments!

[OUTRO — signature loop-out]
```

## Template C — "What If" Scenario

> Best for: space, science, survival hypotheticals. One idea, escalating stakes.

```
[COLD OPEN]
VO: What would happen if [hypothetical]? The answer is stranger — and scarier — than
    you'd think.

[PROMISE]
VO: Let's find out, step by step. And trust me, it gets wild by the end.

[ESCALATION BEATS — each raises the stakes]
Beat 1 (first seconds):  In the first [time], [immediate consequence].
Beat 2 (minutes/hours):  Then, [second-order consequence].
Beat 3 (days/weeks):     After that, [bigger consequence].
Beat 4 (the extreme):    And eventually… [the dramatic climax].

[REASSURING TURN — the "bright side"]
VO: Now, before you panic — [why it won't actually happen / the comforting fact].

[OUTRO — signature loop-out]
```

## Template D — Mystery / Reveal

> Best for: unsolved mysteries, "scientists finally…", historical enigmas.

```
[COLD OPEN]
VO: For [years/centuries], nobody could explain [the mystery]. Until now.

[PROMISE]
VO: Today we're diving into [mystery] — and the theory that might finally solve it.
    Stay to the end for the part that changes everything.

[BUILD — layer the intrigue]
- Set the scene: what/where/when.
- Raise the question: why it doesn't make sense.
- Theory 1: [+ why it's not enough].
- Theory 2: [+ the flaw].
- The best theory / new evidence: [the payoff, held to the end].

[HONEST CLOSE]
VO: Is it the final answer? Maybe not. But it's the closest we've ever come.

[OUTRO — signature loop-out]
```

## Template E — Explainer ("Why does…")

> Best for: everyday curiosity, the body, objects around us.

```
[COLD OPEN]
VO: [Everyday observation]. You've seen it a thousand times. But do you know WHY?

[PROMISE]
VO: The real reason is more clever than you'd expect. Here's what's actually going on.

[BODY]
- The common assumption (and why it's wrong).
- The real mechanism, in plain language + one analogy.
- One surprising bonus fact that deepens the "wow."

[OUTRO — signature loop-out]
```

## Hook Bank (swipe file)

Steal and adapt. Every hook = curiosity gap + "you" + bold promise.

**Impossible-sounding fact**
- "There's a place on Earth where it rains fish — and people just carry on with their day."
- "Your phone is dirtier than a toilet seat. A lot dirtier."

**Direct question**
- "What would happen if you stopped sleeping — forever?"
- "Why do we get goosebumps when there's nothing to be scared of?"

**You-do-this-too**
- "You've been [charging your phone / breathing / drinking water] wrong your whole life."
- "There's a button in your car you've probably never used — and it's a game changer."

**Countdown promise**
- "Only 3% of people can spot the difference in under 10 seconds. Can you?"
- "By the end of this video, you'll be able to [skill]. Let's go."

**Forbidden / secret**
- "Hotels don't want you to know what's really on the TV remote."
- "There's a reason plane windows have that tiny hole — and no one tells you."

**Scale flex**
- "This creature is so big it makes a school bus look like a toy."
- "If this star replaced our Sun, it would swallow every planet up to Jupiter."

**Cliffhanger reveal**
- "They drilled the deepest hole on Earth… then sealed it shut. Here's why."
- "Scientists opened a 2,000-year-old jar — and what was inside still worked."

## Title Bank (packaging formulas)

- `[Number] [Topic] That [Surprising Outcome]`
  → "15 Everyday Things You're Using Wrong"
- `Why [Everyday Thing] [Unexpected Question]`
  → "Why Airplane Food Tastes So Bad"
- `What If [Hypothetical]?`
  → "What If Earth Had Two Moons?"
- `Only [X%] of People Can [Task]`
  → "Only 4% of People Can Pass This Memory Test"
- `[Authority] Finally [Revealed/Found] [Mystery]`
  → "Scientists Finally Figured Out Why We Yawn"
- `You've Been [Doing Thing] Wrong Your Whole Life`
  → "You've Been Cutting Fruit Wrong Your Whole Life"
- `[Number] [Body Part / Object] Secrets Nobody Told You`
  → "9 Belly Button Secrets Nobody Told You"

**Packaging checklist:** ✅ a number or "you" · ✅ a curiosity gap left open · ✅ a bold,
specific outcome · ✅ thumbnail promises the same payoff · ✅ never resolve it in the title.
